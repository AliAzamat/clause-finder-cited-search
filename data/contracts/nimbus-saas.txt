SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT between Nimbus Cloud and Harbor Retail

1. Subscription. Nimbus grants Harbor a non-exclusive right to access the service for the number of seats listed in the Order Form during the subscription term.

2. Data Protection. Nimbus will keep Harbor's customer data encrypted at rest and in transit, and will notify Harbor of any security incident within seventy-two hours of confirming it.

3. Service Levels. Nimbus will make the service available at least 99.9 percent of each month. If it falls short, Harbor receives a service credit of ten percent of that month's fees.

4. Renewal. The subscription renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety days before the term ends.

5. Indemnification. Nimbus will defend Harbor against third-party claims that the service infringes intellectual property rights, and will pay damages finally awarded.

6. Dispute Resolution. The parties will first try to settle any dispute through good-faith negotiation for thirty days. If that fails, the dispute will be resolved by binding arbitration in Chicago, Illinois.
